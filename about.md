I am an **AI Engineer** building production-grade AI systems that sit at the intersection of research ideas and real-world engineering constraints. The goal is systems that are not just accurate, but observable, reproducible, and deployable at scale — across agentic AI, large language models, forecasting, and applied machine learning.

## What I build

- **Agentic AI systems** — multi-agent orchestration, routing, planning, memory, and evaluation.
- **LLM-powered applications** — RAG pipelines, prompt optimization, reranking, and LLM-as-judge evaluation.
- **Forecasting and decision models** for sparse, noisy, and highly imbalanced data.
- **End-to-end ML systems** — from modeling and feature engineering through deployment, monitoring, and iteration.

## What I value

- Reproducibility over demos.
- Clear abstractions over clever hacks.
- Measured evaluation over anecdotal success.
- Incremental design, where each phase can be inspected, validated, or rolled back.

## Current focus

- Prompt compression and example selection — reranking with composite usefulness signals.
- Evaluation, observability, and failure-mode analysis for AI systems in production.
- Research paper reviews that emphasize intuition, limitations, and applied relevance.
